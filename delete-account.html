Delete your Taas Club account

Taas Club · card games for Android

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Taas Club and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and follow the three steps (the last one asks you to type DELETE).
  3. Your account is deleted automatically 24 hours later. Until then a banner lets you cancel and keep everything.

Without the app

Email av.n@aol.com with the subject "Delete my Taas Club account" and your 12-digit player ID (shown in Profile and in Settings). We'll publish a deletion request for your public profile within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • On your phone: your secret key, coins, gems, level, unlocked designs, friends, blocked list and settings.
  • On the Nostr relays: your public profile is replaced with an empty "deleted" profile and a deletion request (Nostr NIP-09) is sent for it.

What may be kept

Nostr relays are run by independent operators. Most honour deletion requests, but some may keep copies of public data — for example past game results, reports or encrypted messages you sent — for their own retention period. Your secret key only ever existed on your phone, so after deletion nobody can sign in as you.

See also the privacy policy.
